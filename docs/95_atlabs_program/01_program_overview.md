## What Is AltaLab?

AltaLab is a free founder acceleration program by **AltaIR Capital** (Igor Ryabenkiy, Managing Partner). It teaches the **Unicorn Focus** methodology — helping founders fix lack of focus, the root cause of most startup failures.

| Stage | Name | Format | Outcome |
|-------|------|--------|---------|
| **1** | Online Sprint | 3 weeks, online (Evolve platform) | Problem clarity, validation, one-pager, MVP roadmap, pitch |
| **2** | Founder Residency | Selected teams; online + in-person | Hands-on mentorship, workshops, Demo Day prep |
| **3** | Build / Scale | Selected teams | $50K SAFE (GTM) or up to $500K from AltaIR Capital |

**Selection for Stage 2** is based on Online Sprint performance — completing assignments and applying frameworks to your real startup.

## What the Sprint Teaches (Public Curriculum Themes)

Based on AltaLab marketing and prior cohorts (Igor Ryabenkiy, *Unicorn Focus*):

| Theme | Application to Your Startup |
|-------|----------------------------|
| **Focus** | One problem, one customer, one bet |
| **Problem definition** | Who hurts, how much, why now |
| **Validation** | Evidence vs. belief; pivot signals |
| **MVP scope** | Minimum lovable product, not feature buffet |
| **Communication** | One-pager, user pitch, investor pitch |
| **Roadmap** | What to build next and what to cut |
| **AI era fundamentals** | Building in 2026 context |

**Deliverables founders typically produce:**
- Clear problem statement and ICP
- Validation evidence (or pivot decision)
- Focused one-pager
- MVP roadmap
- Pitch versions for users and investors
- 90-day execution plan
